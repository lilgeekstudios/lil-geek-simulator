# Lil Geek Simulator 1.2.1

- Contacts are much easier to find on drop offs once you're ready to meet them: Lu Twan after 5 finished drop offs with $200 on you, Mr. Whitlock after 30 with $1,000.
- The drop offs panel tells you what you still need before you can meet the next contact.
- The drop offs, packaging and trade up column scrolls if it doesn't all fit.

## Download
1. Click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it anywhere.
3. Run **LilGeekSimulator.exe**. Keep the **Audio** folder next to it: that's where the game's sounds and music live.

Your save is kept separately (in %AppData%\CaseOpener), so updating never touches it.
